Audigue - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Audigue ("the extension") is a browser extension that reads the text content of web pages, Google Docs and Notion pages aloud using text-to-speech. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

The short version

Audigue has no servers of its own. It does not collect, store or transmit your personal data to the developer or to any third party. The only information it saves is your own settings, kept locally in your browser.

What the extension accesses

  • Page text. When you press Play, Audigue reads the visible text of the page you are currently viewing. This text is used only to generate speech and is not stored or sent to the developer.
  • Your preferences. Your reading speed, selected voice and chosen engine are stored locally on your device using the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). They never leave your browser.

How the page text is processed

Audigue offers two text-to-speech engines, and where your text goes depends on your choice:

  • VoiceBox engine (optional). The text is sent to the VoiceBox application running locally on your own computer at http://localhost:17493. It does not leave your machine and is not sent to any remote server.
  • Browser engine, local (on-device) voices. Speech is produced entirely on your device by your browser or operating system. The text does not leave your machine.
  • Browser engine, network voices. Some voices provided by your browser or operating system are "network" voices. When you select one of these, the page text may be sent by your operating system or browser to its voice provider in order to generate the audio. This processing is performed by your system's voice provider, not by Audigue. Audigue labels these voices in its interface so you can choose a local voice if you prefer.

Data sharing and selling

Audigue does not sell or transfer your data to third parties. It does not use your data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to reading pages aloud.

Permissions

  • activeTab - to access the page you are currently viewing, only when you use the extension.
  • scripting - to extract the readable text from the active tab.
  • storage - to remember your preferences locally.
  • host permission (localhost:17493) - to talk to the optional local VoiceBox application.

Children

Audigue is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the extension after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
